Instead of redirecting your customer to a checkout page, you'll be able to ask SayaBeliPay for the DuitNow QR itself and show it on your own page. The customer scans it with their banking app or e-wallet without ever leaving your site.

## How it will work

1. The customer reviews their order on your confirmation page (items, total) and taps **Pay with DuitNow QR**.
2. Only then does your server create a payment with `channel=DUITNOW_QR` and ask for the QR instead of a checkout URL.
3. SayaBeliPay returns the **QR image** and its **expiry time**.
4. You show the QR on your page, either the standard design or a custom design set up with SayaBeliPay (see below).
5. The customer scans and pays in their app.
6. You confirm the result by [polling the transaction status](/guides/payment-status), the same as for any other payment.

<Tip>
  Create the QR only when the customer confirms, not when they open the cart or the checkout page. The QR's 5 minutes start the moment it's created, so making it early wastes the customer's time and leaves unused payments behind.
</Tip>

## Rules for the QR

| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Valid for 5 minutes** | Show a countdown. When it runs out, hide the QR and offer a "Try again" button. |
| **One payment only** | Each QR can be paid once. To retry after it expires, create a **new payment with a new `order_no`**. |
| **Amount is fixed** | The amount is built into the QR. The customer can't change it in their app. |
| **Don't alter the QR** | The QR is digitally signed. Editing or redrawing it makes banking apps reject it. For a branded look, request a [custom design](#custom-qr-design). |

## Custom QR design

The standard QR works for every account. If you'd like the QR to match your brand, **contact SayaBeliPay to request a custom design**, and we'll set it up and test it with you. Please send us:

- your business name as it should appear on the page
- your logo (PNG or SVG)
- your brand colour

**What can be customised:**
- your business name, logo and colours around the QR
- the colour of the QR code itself, as long as it stays **dark on a light background** (so every banking app can read it)
- a small logo in the centre of the QR

**What can't be changed:** the name the customer's app shows after scanning. Banking apps read that name from inside the QR, which is digitally signed, so they will always show **CHIP IN SDN. BHD.**, our payment processor, not your business name.

<Warning>
  Customers who see your name on the page but a different name in their banking app may think something is wrong and stop paying. Always add a line under the QR such as:

  *"Payment processed securely by CHIP IN SDN. BHD."*
</Warning>

## What to show the customer

A clear QR page usually has:

- your business name and logo
- the amount, e.g. **RM 25.00**
- the QR code
- a countdown, e.g. "Expires in 04:32"
- a short instruction, e.g. "Scan with any banking or e-wallet app that supports DuitNow QR"
- the line "Payment processed securely by CHIP IN SDN. BHD."
- a "QR expired, try again" state for when the countdown ends
