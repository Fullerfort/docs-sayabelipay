---
title: Payment Channels
description: What to put in the `channel` field
---

The `channel` field on [`POST /api/payment`](/api-reference/create-payment) tells SayaBeliPay how the customer wants to pay. The customer is then sent to a checkout page that only offers that method.

## Available now

| `channel` | Method | What the customer does |
|---|---|---|
| `FPX` | FPX Online Banking (personal bank accounts) | Picks their bank on the checkout page and logs in to their bank to approve. |
| `DUITNOW_QR` | DuitNow QR | Scans a QR code with their banking app or e-wallet. |

`ONLINE_BANKING` is accepted as an alias for `FPX`.

<Note>
  DuitNow QR payments **cannot be refunded** through SayaBeliPay. See [Refund a Payment](/api-reference/refund).
</Note>

## Coming soon

| `channel` | Method | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `CC` | Credit / debit card (Visa, Mastercard) | Awaiting card-scheme approval |
| `GOOGLE_PAY` | Google Pay | Awaiting approval |

Until these are enabled on your account, sending them returns the "not currently available" response below. You don't need to change anything when they go live — the same `channel` values will start working.

## Channel not available

If you send a `channel` value that isn't enabled on your account, no checkout page is created and you get back:

```json
{
  "status": 400,
  "message": "The selected payment channel is not currently available."
}
```

The same response is returned if a method is temporarily unavailable for the amount you sent (some methods have their own minimums).
