title: FPX Bank Codes
description: Reference codes for the optional payment_code field

These are the standard FPX bank codes, for accounts that support skipping the bank-selection page (see [Payment Channels](/guides/payment-channels)). Always confirm which banks are actually online **right now** via [`GET /api/fpx_bank_list`](/api-reference/fpx-bank-list) rather than assuming from this table — individual banks go offline for maintenance from time to time, and this call reflects live status.
